TopCashback Review: Straight Cash, Competitive Rates

By Tamésco · Updated November 28, 2026

TopCashback is a free cashback program known for passing a large share of its commissions back to members. It is a good fit if you want plain cash and like to compare rates.

Referral note: I may earn a reward if you join TopCashback through the links on this page. That does not change what you get, and this review is my own assessment based on TopCashback's public information.

How TopCashback works

You join for free, search for the store you want on TopCashback's site, app or browser extension, and click through before buying. The cashback appears as pending, then becomes payable once the store confirms the purchase after its return window.

Why its rates are often high

TopCashback's model is to pass most of the commission it receives back to members, so at many stores its rate is among the highest you'll find. That is not true for every store on every day, so it's worth comparing with other programs on bigger purchases.

How withdrawals work

Once cashback is payable, you choose when to withdraw it, for example to your bank account or PayPal. TopCashback also offers gift card options. The site says there's no minimum withdrawal amount; check its current payment page for details.

Is TopCashback legit?

Yes. TopCashback is an established cashback program that operates in the US and other countries and works with a large number of well-known retailers. As with any cashback program, rewards depend on tracking and on the store confirming the sale.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Free to join
  • Cashback rates that are often among the highest
  • Withdraw payable cashback when you want
  • Paid as straight cash (bank or PayPal)

Cons

  • The site is more functional than polished
  • Usually no large sign-up bonus
  • No transferable points option like Rakuten's
  • Rates change, so the "top" rate isn't guaranteed

Who TopCashback suits best

  • Shoppers who want cash, not points
  • People who compare rates before bigger purchases
  • Anyone who wants to decide when to withdraw

Verdict

If maximizing straight cashback is your priority, TopCashback deserves a place in your toolkit, and it works well alongside Rakuten. See Rakuten vs TopCashback for a side-by-side comparison.
