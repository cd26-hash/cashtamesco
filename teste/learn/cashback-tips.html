10 Tips to Get More Cashback on Online Purchases

By Tamésco · Updated November 28, 2026

This guide contains referral links. If you join a program through them, I may earn a reward at no extra cost to you. Disclosure

Most of the value in cashback comes from a few simple habits. These ten tips help your purchases track reliably and make sure you get the best available rate.

  1. Compare rates before bigger purchases

    The same store can pay different rates on different programs. Before a large order, check Rakuten, TopCashback and Rove Miles side by side.

  2. Install the browser extension

    Program extensions remind you when cashback is available and activate it with one click, so you don't forget.

  3. Always start at the program

    Make it a habit to click through from the cashback program, even for stores you visit often. See how tracking works.

  4. Read the store's terms

    Each store lists exclusions, such as gift cards or certain brands. A minute of reading avoids surprises later.

  5. Use only approved coupons

    Codes from outside coupon sites can cancel your cashback. Use coupons shown by the program or the store itself.

  6. Watch for seasonal promotions

    Rates often go up around big shopping events like Black Friday, Cyber Monday and back-to-school. If a purchase can wait, it may earn more then.

  7. Check that it tracked

    Look for the pending entry in your account a few days after buying. If it's missing after a week, file a claim.

  8. Avoid unnecessary returns and cancellations

    Returned or cancelled items lose their cashback.

  9. Keep your order confirmations

    You'll need the order number, date and amount if you ever file a missing-cashback claim.

  10. Don't buy just for the reward

    Cashback is a discount on what you were going to buy anyway, not a reason to spend more.
