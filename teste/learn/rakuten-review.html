Rakuten Review: Cashback or Points on Everyday Shopping

By Tamésco · Updated November 28, 2026

This guide contains referral links. If you join a program through them, I may earn a reward at no extra cost to you. Disclosure

Rakuten (formerly Ebates) is one of the best-known cashback programs in the US. Its standout feature is that you can choose to collect cash or transferable points. Here is what to know before you join.

Referral note: I may earn a reward if you join Rakuten through the links on this page. That does not change what you get, and the pros and cons below are my own assessment based on Rakuten's public information.

How Rakuten works

You join for free, then start your shopping at Rakuten's website, app or browser extension. Rakuten shows the current cashback rate for each store. After you buy, the reward appears in your account as pending and is confirmed after the store's return window.

Cash or points

By default Rakuten pays cash back. Members who have an eligible American Express card can switch their account to earn Amex Membership Rewards points instead, and Rakuten has also offered a Bilt Rewards option. For people who already collect those points for travel, this can make Rakuten more valuable than a cash-only program. Check Rakuten's current terms for which cards and programs are eligible.

How you get paid

Rakuten pays on a periodic schedule rather than on demand, by PayPal or check (or as points if you switched to Amex/Bilt). A minimum balance applies. If you prefer to withdraw whenever you like, TopCashback may suit you better.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Free, and very easy to use
  • Large network of participating stores
  • Browser extension that reminds you when cashback is available
  • Option to earn Amex Membership Rewards or Bilt points
  • New-member welcome bonus (terms set by Rakuten)

Cons

  • Rates are not always the highest at every store
  • Payouts follow Rakuten's schedule, not yours
  • Points options need an eligible card or account
  • Rates and promotions change often

About the welcome bonus

New members who join through a referral link usually get a welcome bonus after a qualifying purchase. Rakuten sets the amount, the minimum spend and the deadline, and these change from time to time. The current offer is shown on Rakuten's sign-up page.

Who Rakuten suits best

  • People who collect Amex Membership Rewards or Bilt points
  • Beginners who want the simplest experience
  • Shoppers who like a browser extension doing the work

Verdict

Rakuten is a strong first cashback program, especially if you value points for travel. If your only goal is the highest cash rate, compare it with TopCashback store by store. See Rakuten vs TopCashback.
