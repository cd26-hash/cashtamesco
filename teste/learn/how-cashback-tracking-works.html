How Cashback Tracking Works

By Tamésco · Updated November 28, 2026

Almost every "missing cashback" problem comes down to tracking. Understanding how a store knows you came from a cashback program makes it much easier to get credited every time.

The basics: clicks, codes and cookies

When you click from a cashback program to a store, the link passes through an affiliate network that adds a tracking code. The store saves that code, usually in a cookie in your browser, for a limited time. When you place an order, the store checks for the code and, if it finds it, reports the sale and the commission to the program.

Three things have to be true for this to work: the tracking code has to reach the store, it has to still be there when you check out, and nothing else can replace it before you pay.

What breaks tracking

  • Ad blockers and strict privacy settings. Some block affiliate redirects or delete cookies. Allow the cashback site and the store while you shop, or use the program's app.
  • Clicking another link afterwards. If you click a coupon site, a price-comparison link or a social media ad after the cashback link, that site's code can replace the cashback one.
  • Unapproved coupon codes. Many stores treat a code from an outside coupon site as a different referral and won't pay cashback.
  • Switching devices or apps. Clicking on your laptop and buying in the store's phone app usually loses the code.
  • Waiting too long. Tracking codes expire. If you clicked yesterday, click through again before you buy.
  • Items added before the click. Some stores only credit a cart created after you arrive from the program.

Pending, confirmed, paid

After the store reports a sale, it appears in your account as pending. That can take from a few hours to several days. It stays pending until the store's return window is over, then becomes confirmed. Only confirmed cashback can be paid out. If you return part of an order, the cashback is usually adjusted to match.

Habits that keep tracking reliable

  1. Start every shopping session at the cashback program, not at the store.
  2. Use one browser on one device from click to checkout.
  3. Empty your cart first, then add items after you click through.
  4. Use only coupons listed by the cashback program or the store itself.
  5. Complete the purchase in the same session, without clicking other promotional links.
  6. Save the order confirmation email until the cashback is paid.

If a purchase doesn't show up

Give it a few days, since some stores report slowly. If it is still missing after about a week, file a missing-cashback claim through the program's help pages with your order number, date and amount. Tamésco can't see or change your account, so claims always go directly to Rakuten, TopCashback or Rove Miles.

Quick answers

Why didn't my cashback track?

The most common reasons are an ad blocker, clicking another site's link or coupon after the cashback link, switching devices, or the tracking code expiring before checkout.

How long until a purchase shows as pending?

Usually within a few hours to several days, depending on how quickly the store reports sales.
