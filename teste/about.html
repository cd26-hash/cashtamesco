About Tamésco

Tamésco is a small, independent website about cashback, shopping rewards and travel rewards. It is run by an individual who uses these programs for everyday online shopping and wanted a simple, honest place to explain how they work and how they compare.

What this site is for

The goal is to help you understand cashback and rewards programs before you sign up, and to choose the one that fits how you shop. Right now the site covers Rakuten (good if you collect Amex Membership Rewards or Bilt points), TopCashback (good for straight cashback) and Rove Miles (good if you prefer miles for travel). The guides explain how cashback works, how tracking works and how the programs compare.

How programs are evaluated

Comparisons and reviews on this site are based on each program's public information and on my own experience using them. I look at:

  • How easy the program is to use
  • How rewards are paid (cash, points or miles) and how payouts work
  • How many stores participate
  • How clear the terms are
  • Overall value for a typical shopper

No program can pay to be described more favorably, and drawbacks are listed alongside the benefits.

How this site makes money

The links to Rakuten, TopCashback and Rove Miles are referral links. If you join through them, I may receive a bonus, commission or reward. It costs you nothing extra and does not reduce what you receive. Links like these are marked as sponsored, and referral relationships are disclosed on every page. Full details are in Terms & Disclosure.

Accuracy and updates

Cashback rates, bonuses, participating stores and program terms change, sometimes without notice. I update the site when I notice changes, but always check the current terms on the program's own site before signing up. If you spot something out of date, please let me know.

What this site is not

Cashback is a discount on purchases you already plan to make. It is not a way to earn an income, and nothing on this site is financial advice.
