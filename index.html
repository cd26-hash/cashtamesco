Questions

Is it free to join?

Yes. Rakuten, TopCashback and Rove Miles are all free to join and free to use.

Rakuten, TopCashback or Rove Miles: which one should I choose?

Choose Rakuten if you collect Amex Membership Rewards or Bilt points, or want the bigger sign-up bonus. Choose TopCashback if you want straight cash and often the highest cashback rates. Choose Rove Miles if you would rather earn miles for flights and hotel stays.

Can I join all three?

Yes. You can join Rakuten, TopCashback and Rove Miles at the same time, then check which one pays more at a given store before buying. Each purchase earns rewards from only one of them.

How do I get the sign-up bonus?

Join through the link on this page, then make a qualifying purchase through the program. Each program sets its own minimum spend, time limit and payout rules, so check their terms when you sign up.

How does cashback work?

Stores pay these programs a commission when you shop through them. They share part of it with you as cashback, points or miles. You start at the program's site or browser extension, then shop at the store as usual.

Why use the links on this site?

They are referral links. For Rakuten and Rove Miles they unlock the new-member bonus. I may also earn a referral commission or reward, at no extra cost to you.
