There is no single winner. Rakuten is usually the better choice if you want points or the simplest experience, TopCashback if you want the highest straight cash, and Rove Miles if you'd rather earn travel miles. Here is the detail.

Choose Rakuten if…

  • You collect Amex Membership Rewards or Bilt points
  • You want the simplest experience and a good browser extension
  • You want a welcome bonus on your first qualifying purchase

Choose TopCashback if…

  • You want plain cash
  • You compare rates before buying
  • You want to withdraw on your own schedule

Choose Rove Miles if…

  • You'd rather earn miles than cash
  • You plan to redeem for flights or hotel stays

Can you use more than one?

Yes, and many experienced shoppers do. You can join all three, then check which one pays the most at a given store before you buy. Each purchase earns from only one program, the last one you clicked through before checkout, so don't click through more than one for the same order.

Verdict

For most people, the best setup is Rakuten plus TopCashback, checking both before larger purchases. Add Rove Miles if travel rewards matter more to you than cash. Read the full Rakuten review and TopCashback review for more detail.

Quick answers

Is Rakuten or TopCashback better?

Neither is better for everyone. Rakuten suits people who want Amex/Bilt points or the simplest experience; TopCashback suits people who want the highest straight cashback and to withdraw when they choose.

Can I use Rakuten and TopCashback at the same time?

You can be a member of both, but each purchase earns from only one: the last program you clicked through before checkout.
