What Is Cashback and How Does It Work?

By Tamésco · Updated November 28, 2026

Cashback is a shopping reward: when you buy from a participating store through a cashback program, you get a share of the purchase back as cash, points or miles. Here is how it works, who pays for it, and what to watch out for.

Who pays for cashback?

The store does. Retailers pay cashback programs such as Rakuten and TopCashback a commission when a shopper they referred buys something. It is a form of advertising: instead of paying for an ad up front, the store pays only when a sale actually happens.

The cashback program then shares part of that commission with you. That is why cashback is usually a percentage of what you spend (for example, 3% at one store and 8% at another) and why the rate is different at each store and changes over time.

How it works, step by step

  1. Join a cashback program. Membership in the programs covered on this site is free.
  2. Start at the program. Before you shop, click through to the store from the program's website or app, or activate its browser extension.
  3. Shop as usual. You pay the store's normal price. Nothing changes at checkout.
  4. The store reports the sale. The program records your purchase as pending.
  5. The reward is confirmed. After the store's return window passes, the pending amount becomes confirmed (sometimes called payable).
  6. You cash out. Depending on the program, you receive cash by PayPal, bank transfer or check, or rewards as points or miles.

How a purchase is tracked

When you click through from a cashback program, the store's site receives a tracking code that tells it where you came from. When you complete the order, the store matches the sale to that code and reports it. This is why it matters to start at the program every time: if the code is missing, the store doesn't know to pay a commission, and there is nothing to share with you.

For the details, including the most common reasons tracking fails, see How Cashback Tracking Works.

How long does it take to get paid?

Expect to wait. A purchase usually shows up as pending within a few days, but it is typically confirmed only after the store's return period is over, which is often 30 to 90 days. After that, each program pays on its own schedule. Rakuten, for example, sends payments on a regular periodic schedule, while TopCashback lets you withdraw confirmed cashback when you choose. Check each program's payment page for the current rules.

Why cashback is sometimes declined

  • You returned or cancelled all or part of the order.
  • You used a coupon code that the store or program doesn't allow with cashback.
  • The product category is excluded (gift cards and some electronics often are).
  • The click wasn't tracked, for example because an ad blocker or another site's link replaced the tracking code.
  • The offer was for new customers only, and the store recognized you as an existing customer.

Most programs let you file a missing-cashback claim if a purchase never shows up. Keep your order confirmation until the reward is paid.

Cashback vs. coupons

A coupon lowers the price at checkout. Cashback gives you money back after the purchase. They can often be combined, as long as the coupon comes from the cashback program itself or the store's own promotions. Codes from unrelated coupon sites can override the tracking and cancel the cashback.

Is cashback worth it?

For purchases you were already going to make, cashback is a simple way to get a little back. It is not a way to earn an income, and it is never worth buying something only for the reward: 5% back on something you didn't need is still 95% spent. Used as a habit on normal shopping, it adds up over a year.

Quick answers

Is cashback free money?

No. It is a share of the commission a store pays the cashback program when you buy something. You only get it on purchases you actually make, and it can be reversed if you return the item.

How long does cashback take?

A purchase usually shows as pending within a few days and is confirmed after the store's return window, often 30 to 90 days. Each program then pays on its own schedule.

Can I use a coupon and cashback together?

Often yes, if the coupon comes from the cashback program or the store itself. Codes from unrelated coupon sites can replace the tracking and cancel the cashback.
